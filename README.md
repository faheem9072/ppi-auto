# PPI AUTO – Premium Website Redesign Concept
**Digital Agency Presentation Prototype prepared by KORE**

A website redesign concept for **PPI Auto** ([prepurchaseauto.com](https://prepurchaseauto.com/)), inspired by the Behance automotive design reference.

---

## 🏎️ Design System & Philosophy

- **Canvas & Palette**: Warm sand / ecru canvas (`#F4EFEB`, `#FAF7F2`) framed within dark obsidian/carbon structural cards (`#111215`, `#181A20`), with vivid Racing Red (`#E5192D`) accents.
- **Architectural Typography**: High-impact condensed display headings with red indicator bars (`|`) and triple red slashes (`///`).
- **Hero Vehicle Focal Centerpiece**: Red SUV cutout with ground contact shadow, subtle watermark typography, and interactive diagnostic hotspots.
- **Interactive Before / After Defect Slider**: Interactive comparison between surface appearance and PPI Auto's 122-point diagnostic discoveries.
- **Interactive 122-Point Inspection Explorer**: Searchable and filterable by system (Engine, OBD-II, Frame & Body, Brakes, Road Test, Interior).
- **Interactive Dossier Modal**: Full sample inspection report with OBD-II diagnostic readouts, ultrasonic paint depth maps (mils), and high-resolution photos.
- **Interactive 3-Step Booking Modal**: Vehicle selection, seller coordination, and transparent $225 flat-rate summary.
